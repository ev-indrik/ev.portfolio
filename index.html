About me

I am a motivated Front-End Developer with 6 months of hands-on experience creating interactive, user-friendly web interfaces. Proficient in React, JavaScript, CSS, and HTML, and skilled in building modern web applications
I took my first steps in programming during my childhood on a Sinclair computer. This adds a bit of an unconventional twist to the usual story 😊

Starting in December 2024, I am an intern at a startup with an international team, where I develop a frontend part of a web platform using React, TypeScript, and AntDesign. My responsibilities include creating responsive and visually appealing pages based on Figma designs, collaborating closely with the designer, and addressing various inquiries throughout development. I use Git for version control to ensure smooth project management and team collaboration.

While working in another profession, I was involved in web development/layout and administration (filling and changing content through admin panels), learning technical details along the way as I completed tasks. Although my responsibilities were not directly related to this field (personal assistant, moderator, online support operator, copywriter, translator in office, remote, and freelance work), some skills have come in handy

My present role:

  • Creating responsive web pages based on Figma designs and implementing adaptive layouts for all screen sizes.
  • Building and integrating user registration, authentication, and dynamic form flows, including backend (Strapi) and HubSpot CRM connections.
  • Working with Mapbox and Turf.js to develop interactive maps with drawing, file uploads, and automated geospatial calculations.
  • Collaborating with REST APIs, customizing backend content structures, and contributing to backend logic.
  • Independently managing feature development, version control (Git), and UI/UX improvements.
